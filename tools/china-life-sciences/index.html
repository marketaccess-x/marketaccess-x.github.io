Scope, evidence and outstanding checks
What is included

Pharmaceutical and biotechnology R&D, clinical trial and manufacturing activity in mainland China and Hong Kong, at the level of the operating firm and site. Medical devices are outside the scope of this map.

Restriction labels

"Subject to now" means a measure is in force and names or covers the firm. "Listed (enjoined)" marks a designation that a court has preliminarily enjoined but whose commercial effect has already been felt. "Pending measure" means a proposed measure covers the firm's activity or modality, or a designation is awaited. No label is inferred.

Clinical trials

City records count interventional drug and biologic trials with at least one site in the city that started between 1 January 2023 and 31 December 2025, as registered on ClinicalTrials.gov and retrieved on 28 October 2026 (9,820 trials in all). A multicenter trial counts once in each city where it has a site, so city totals overlap. Trials registered only on China's own drug trial registry are not included, so domestic activity is understated. Hospital counts are site entries; placeholder entries such as "Research Site" are excluded. Cities with fewer than 100 trials are not shown.

What remains unresolved

Ownership classification for firms headquartered or redomiciled abroad, exact site addresses and the modality of undisclosed programs are marked rather than inferred. Coordinates are city centroids, offset slightly where a city holds several records. "Location basis" in each record says whether the city is stated in a source opened in this review or taken from the company's own description.
