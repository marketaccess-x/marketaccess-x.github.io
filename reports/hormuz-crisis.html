Key findings

Finding 01

Six weeks in, no exit is visible. Iran rejected the US 15-point peace plan on March 25. Military escalation has continued; the UNSC is deadlocked. The disruption should be planned for in months, not weeks.

Finding 02

Energy markets are in an escalating crisis trajectory. Physical Brent hit $141/bbl on April 2 — a $32 spread over futures signalling extreme physical scarcity. The IEA warns April will be worse than March.

Finding 03

Qatar's LNG shutdown is now a multi-year structural problem. A second Iranian strike wave on March 18–19 caused damage assessed at 3–5 years to repair. Force majeure has been extended through mid-June.

Finding 04

The fertilizer shock is locking in agricultural damage that cannot be reversed. Urea at $700–720/MT. The kharif planting window is closing. The WFP projects 45 million additional people pushed into acute hunger by mid-2026.

Finding 05

Petrochemical supply chains are fracturing across Asia. 31+ force majeures declared. Naphtha up ~100% to $1,050–1,300/MT. Cracker utilisation at 53–60%. Downstream consequences reach packaging, medical devices, automotive, and construction.

Finding 06

Financial contagion is spreading at record pace. The Indian rupee hit a record low of 95.20/USD. The Korean won broke 1,500. Gold at $4,720+/oz. The IMF, IEA, and World Bank have activated a joint crisis mechanism.

Finding 07

Financial infrastructure failed before physical blockade. The withdrawal of P&I war-risk insurance created an economic blockade before Iran enforced a physical one. The $20bn DFC-Chubb reinsurance facility has not restored commercial traffic.

Finding 08 — New

Aviation fuel is the next chokepoint. Jet fuel has doubled to $197/bbl. Southeast Asian carriers are cutting routes through September. European airports face dry-out risk within weeks. The summer travel season and air freight corridors are directly at risk.
