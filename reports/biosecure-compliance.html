⏱ Three clocks are running

The BIOSECURE Act became law on December 18, 2025. It names no companies. Instead, it creates two separate regulatory tracks that determine which Chinese biotechnology firms become prohibited counterparties for federal contracts and grants, and when that prohibition takes effect. WuXi AppTec is not yet designated under either track. That gap is not a reprieve. It is a planning window — and as of April 2, 2026, a third clock started running independently.

The analytical problem this report solves

Most coverage of the BIOSECURE Act treats it as a binary: either the prohibition has bitten, or it hasn’t. That framing is wrong and it is causing companies to mismanage their compliance timelines. The Act creates two designation tracks with different speeds, different procedural protections, and different FAR trigger timelines. Which track a firm is on determines its planning window. Which track your supply chain exposure runs through determines yours.

WuXi AppTec — the world’s largest CRDMO and a counterparty to an estimated 30% of FDA-approved biologics in development — is not yet designated. It was briefly added to the DoD 1260H list in February 2026 and withdrawn within one hour. It remains on OMB time. That distinction is the difference between a 60-day compliance window and a multi-year repositioning window — and most affected companies have not mapped which one applies to them.

Key findings

Finding 01

The BIOSECURE Act is law — but its prohibitions are not yet in effect. FAR revision is required before any prohibition bites. The OMB initial BCC list is due December 18, 2026. FAR revision follows no earlier than mid-2028.

Finding 02

The dual-track designation mechanism is the key analytical variable. The DoD 1260H track is fast, opaque, and carries no pre-designation notice rights. The OMB track is slower, procedural, and gives affected firms a 90-day response window. WuXi is on OMB time.

Finding 03

WuXi’s repositioning is a geographic hedge, not a clean break. Non-China capacity in Singapore, Delaware, Ireland, and Switzerland is being built in parallel with accelerating U.S. revenue growth. The hedge is real but the dependency is deepening simultaneously.

Finding 04

The downstream contractor cascade is the most underappreciated provision. Section 851(a)(2) bars federal contractors from using BCC services in performing a federal contract — sweeping in subcontractors, sub-tier suppliers, and grant recipients who never contracted with WuXi directly.

Finding 05

A third clock started April 2, 2026. President Trump’s Section 232 executive order imposes 100% tariffs on imported patented pharmaceuticals and APIs, effective July 31 for large companies. This regime operates independently of BIOSECURE, moves faster, and creates overlapping commercial pressure on the same restructuring decisions.

Finding 06

No legal challenges to the enacted BIOSECURE Act have been filed. The shift from naming specific companies to an executive-branch designation process resolved the bill-of-attainder constitutional vulnerability that attracted industry opposition to earlier drafts. Litigation risk has not disappeared — it has migrated to the designation phase.
